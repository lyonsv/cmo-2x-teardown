# Phase 03.1: Headline Metric Reframe - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-24
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A CMO reads the 2x claim in terms they already use. The claim becomes twice the campaign-markets in market per quarter, at flat budget and headcount, with brief-to-live time and cost per approved asset beside it. Variant counts become an internal factory and capacity measure.

**Why:** the user challenged "approved-live variants per marketing head". There is no CMO practice or benchmark for it, and most variants are crops, sizes and locales, so the count is mechanical and easy to game. The metric came from translating Intercom's "merged PRs per R&D head" literally into marketing.

Touches the answer paragraph, s01 (metric, trio, six-lever table, falsifier, "does not mean"), s02 (framing, Figure 1 and table), s05/s07 (relabel variants as internal throughput), s10 scorecard wording, s11 calculator (inputs, outputs, summary, chart, believe panel, maths), s15 registers, data.js, calc/model/state code and tests. Requirements: CRED-03 (revised), CRED-05, CFO-02, CFO-04, LEVER-06, CALC-01, CALC-02.

Phase 4 plans 04-07 (CDP a11y/print scripts) can run before or after this phase. 04-08/04-09 (preview, publish) are held until this phase completes.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### What counts as the 2x
- Headline metric: **campaign-markets in market per quarter**. One campaign live in one market = 1, so the same campaign in 6 markets = 6. It is reported as an org total at flat budget and flat headcount, with no per-head denominator anywhere in the headline.
- Companions shown beside it: **brief-to-live time** (median days) and **cost per approved asset**.
- Variant counts appear only in s05 (factory) and s07 (review capacity), labelled "derived variants: internal throughput, not the claim". s01 states plainly that extra crops, sizes or locales of the same campaign-market do not count toward 2x.
- "Variants per marketing head" no longer appears as a claim anywhere on the page. Any remaining per-head figure must be clearly secondary or removed.
- The falsifier and "what 2x does not mean" are restated against campaign-markets. The falsifier keeps the user's OR structure: if campaign-markets double but holdout-measured conversion/CAC does not improve, or first-pass compliance falls, within 2–3 quarters, the claim is false for that org.

### Model, calculator and delivery
- The 2x scenario doubles **campaigns × markets**. A new calculator input, "how you get to 2x", has three options: more campaigns / more markets / both. The default is both, about √2 each. Variants, review load, cost and AI spend follow mechanically from campaign-markets.
- Brief-to-live time reuses the existing cycle-time model (currently 11.6 → 7.2 days), renamed "brief-to-live" and graded Modelled.
- CFO companions: keep **cost per approved asset**, and add **production cost per campaign-market** in s02 and the calculator.
- Variants-per-head claims, tier-weighted per-head sensitivity and per-head chart panels are replaced or removed. Every new number goes through data.js, gets a register row and a grade chip, and is drift-tested. No test is loosened.
- Checkpoints: the user is back, so the local preview and approve-push checkpoints go to the user. No auto-approval.

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact copy, figure geometry and how the "how you get to 2x" split maps onto existing calc inputs (campaigns per year, locales/markets, concepts per campaign), provided the default reproduces "2× campaign-markets".

</decisions>
