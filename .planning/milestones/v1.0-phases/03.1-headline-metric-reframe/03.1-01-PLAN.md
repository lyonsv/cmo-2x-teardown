## Phase Goal

**As a** CMO (and my CFO), **I want to** read the 2x claim as twice the campaigns live across markets per quarter at flat budget and headcount, with brief-to-live time and cost per approved asset beside it, **so that** the claim is in terms I already manage and cannot be inflated by mechanical crops, sizes or locales.

<objective>
Replace the variants-per-head engine in site/js/calc.js with a campaign-market engine and a "how you get to 2x" growth share, test-first, with hand-calculated goldens.

Purpose: every later plan (data, calculator, page) reads its numbers from compute(); the model change has to be right and pinned first.
Output: new compute() contract (below) and a rewritten test/calc.test.js.
</objective>
