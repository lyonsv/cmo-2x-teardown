CMO 2x: what doubling a marketing organisation's output would take

A sceptical look at what it would take for a large marketing organisation to double its approved output without doubling its cost, and how a CFO could check each step.

A marketing organisation can roughly double approved-and-live asset-variants per marketing head per quarter, from 83 Modelled to 167 Modelled, at a flat budget. Gartner puts spend at 7.7% Benchmark of revenue, so the extra output must come from the same money. That holds only if review is governed: a tiered gate, a review agent and an eval harness. The falsifier: the claim is false if output doubles but holdout-measured conversion and CAC do not improve and first-pass compliance falls within 2–3 quarters.

Claim & measurement

The headline metric counts approved-and-live asset-variants per marketing head per quarter. The denominator is all marketing FTE: brand, operations, regional and agency-equivalent staff. Assets that are generated but not approved never count. The number is always shown beside unique concepts and variants per concept, so growth from new ideas cannot hide behind growth from cheap copies.

The count is of approved-and-live variants only. To reach it the model derives more variants than it publishes, at an approval yield of 95% Modelled; the next section costs the rejected ones.

What 2x does not mean

It does not mean twice as many assets generated, twice the budget, or half the team. It means twice as many approved-and-live asset-variants per marketing head per quarter, at flat budget, with outcomes that hold.

Falsifier

If approved-live variants per head double but holdout-measured conversion/CAC does not improve and first-pass compliance falls within 2–3 quarters, the 2x claim is false for that org.
