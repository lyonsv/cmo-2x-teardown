CMO 2x: what doubling a marketing organisation's output would take

Vincent Lyons

This page is a projection, not a report of something that has happened. Marketing budgets sit at about 7.7% Benchmark of company revenue, so doubling output has to come from the same money. Under the stated assumptions, a governed 2x moves approved-and-live asset-variants per marketing head per quarter from 83 Modelled to 167 Modelled. Every number carries a grade; follow its chip to the source or assumption behind it.
